Knotty

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 September 2026

Knotty is a puzzle game made by Acloe Technology Ventures ("we"). This policy explains what the game collects, why, and how you can delete it. Questions: write to the developer email on Knotty's Google Play or App Store page.

What we collect

  • A player account. When you first open Knotty it creates an anonymous account (a random ID) so your scores can appear on leaderboards. No sign-in is needed.
  • Sign-in details, if you choose to sign in. If you sign in with Google or Apple, we receive your account identifier and the email address that provider shares, so you can use the same account on other devices.
  • Your leaderboard name and scores. A player name (chosen at random, which you can change) and your best score and time on each puzzle board. These are shown publicly on the in-game leaderboards.
  • Crash reports. If the app crashes or hits an error, it sends a technical report: what went wrong, the app version, and the device model and operating system version. Reports don't include your name, email or other personal details.

Your game progress (levels, stars, streaks, Thread balance, collectibles and settings) is stored on your device only.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell your data or share it with advertisers.
  • Knotty currently shows no ads and doesn't track you across other apps or websites. If that changes, we'll update this policy first.
  • We don't access your contacts, photos, location or microphone.

Services we use

These providers process data on our behalf, only to run the game:

  • Supabase: hosts player accounts, names and scores.
  • Sentry: receives crash reports (stored in the EU).
  • Google and Apple: sign-in, if you choose to use it.
  • Shorebird: delivers app updates.

All data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS).

How long we keep it

Your account, name and scores are kept until you delete your account. Crash reports are deleted automatically within 90 days.

Deleting your data

You can delete your account at any time in the app: Settings → Account → Delete account. This immediately and permanently removes your account, leaderboard name and scores. You can also ask us to do it; see Delete your Knotty account. Uninstalling the app removes the progress stored on your device.

Children

Knotty is intended for players aged 13 and over. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we'll delete it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, or to object to how it's used. Write to the developer email on Knotty's Google Play or App Store page and we'll respond within 30 days.

Changes

We'll post any changes to this policy on this page and update the date above.
